About Us

Expert Repairs

Our skilled students repair computers, laptops, tablets, and smartphones using professional-grade tools and techniques.

How Pricing Works

Labor is always free. If a part needs replacing, you just cover the part: screens $40–$200, batteries $20–$60, RAM $20–$80.

Repair Time

Most repairs are done in 2–7 school days. Ordering a part can add extra time.

Tracking Your Repair

Every repair gets a tracking code at drop-off — use it under "Track Repair" anytime to check your status. No account needed.

Where We're Located
